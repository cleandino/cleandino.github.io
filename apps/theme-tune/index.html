Theme Tune

Your music, offline, in the look you pick

Theme Tune plays the music you already own. Bring songs from your own Google Drive or Dropbox, from a computer over Wi-Fi, or from your device, and listen offline — in a spinning CD, an LP turntable, a cassette, a retro 70s hi-fi or an ink-wash scroll.

Key Features

  • 🎨 Five themes that change the whole app — one purchase unlocks them all
  • ☁️ Connect Google Drive or Dropbox, browse your folders and download the songs you choose
  • 📡 Send files from a computer over Wi-Fi, or use iTunes file sharing and the Files app
  • 🎧 Offline playback with lock screen controls, playlists, folders, sleep timer and speed control
  • 🔒 No account and no server of ours — your files stay on your device
