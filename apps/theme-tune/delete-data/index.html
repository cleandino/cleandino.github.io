Data Deletion

Theme Tune by CleanDino

This page explains what data Theme Tune keeps and how to remove it.

We hold no data about you

Theme Tune has no account and no server of ours. Your music files, playlists, folders and playback position are stored only on your own device. We never receive them, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Remove the data from your device

  1. To remove downloaded or imported songs, open the app, go to Settings > Manage storage and delete songs, or choose delete all.
  2. To remove everything the app stores, delete the app from your device. Songs, playlists, folders and settings are deleted with it.
  3. On iOS, files you placed in the app's folder through Finder or the Files app are deleted with the app too.

Disconnect Google Drive or Dropbox

If you connected a cloud account, the app keeps only a sign-in token on your device (in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). It reads your files and never uploads or deletes anything. To remove access:

  1. In the app, open Cloud, tap the menu on the account and choose Disconnect. The token is deleted from your device.
  2. To also revoke the app's access on the service side, open your Google Account (Security > Third-party access) or Dropbox (Settings > Connected apps) and remove Theme Tune.

Advertising and purchases

Ads, when shown, are served by Google AdMob under its own privacy terms; you can change your ad choices in the app's Settings. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store; manage or request refunds there. We do not receive your payment details.
