Data Deletion

Coq French: A1–B2 Vocabulary by CleanDino

This page explains how to delete your data in Coq French: A1–B2 Vocabulary.

Delete your community data in the app

If you joined the community (rankings and follows), you can delete all of that data instantly:

  1. Open the app and go to the Community tab.
  2. Tap the Community Settings (gear) icon at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete community data.
  4. Confirm. Your data is permanently deleted immediately.

What gets deleted

The following data is permanently deleted and is not retained:

  • Your randomly generated nickname and profile emoji
  • Your learning statistics used for rankings
  • Your follow relationships
  • Your push notification token
  • Your anonymous account

Words and corrections you shared

If you turned on Help improve the dictionary, the words you added and the meanings you corrected are sent to help us fix the dictionary. Turn it off in Settings and everything you sent is deleted right away. Deleting a word from My Words, or restoring a meaning, also deletes that item.

Note on anonymous accounts

This app uses anonymous accounts only. We never collect your name, email, phone number, or password. Because accounts are anonymous, the only way to identify and delete your specific data is from within the app. If you have already uninstalled the app, no personally identifiable information remains linked to you on our servers.
